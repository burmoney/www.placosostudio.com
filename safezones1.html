Humanitarian Protected Areas (International Law) In global conflict management, a safe zone is a strictly designated geographic area established via international agreements or demilitarized pacts to protect civilians trapped in active warfare. The Principle: Governed by International Humanitarian Law (IHL), these spaces are neutralized. They are legally barred from holding military objectives or experiencing active hostile operations. Historical Backbone: Pioneered originally by organizations like the International Red Cross to build neutral safe havens for non-combatants, the sick, and the wounded.
